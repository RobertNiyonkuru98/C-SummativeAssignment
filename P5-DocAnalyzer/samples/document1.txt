Internet of Things in the Modern Home

The Internet of Things (IoT) has changed how households manage energy. An IoT
thermostat learns a family's routine and adjusts heating before anyone wakes up.
Each IoT device reports to a hub, and the hub decides when to act.

Security remains the biggest concern for any IoT deployment. A poorly secured
IoT camera can expose a whole home network. Manufacturers now sign firmware
updates, and some IoT standards require encrypted local traffic.

The same technology powers industrial monitoring, where thousands of IoT
sensors measure temperature, pressure and vibration on a factory floor.
