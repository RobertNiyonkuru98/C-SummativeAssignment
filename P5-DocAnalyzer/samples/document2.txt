Cloud Computing and Edge Devices

Cloud platforms provide storage, compute and managed databases on demand.
A typical application moves static content to a content delivery network and
keeps business logic in a managed container service.

Edge computing pushes some of that work closer to the user. A single IoT
gateway, for example, can filter sensor readings locally and only upload
summaries, which reduces bandwidth and latency.

Most teams combine both approaches: cloud for durable state, edge for
latency-sensitive decisions.
